About me

I completed my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2026. Studying circuits, signals and communication gave me a solid base in how hardware behaves. What holds my attention most is the point where hardware meets software: firmware that reads a sensor, a controller that drives a motor, a model that runs on a small board instead of a server.

My final-year project applied a Vision Transformer to image denoising, which pushed me toward computer vision and toward the question of what it takes to run AI on constrained devices. Alongside that, I am learning embedded C and C++, microcontrollers and Raspberry Pi, and practising robotics with Arduino.

I am early in my career and treat every project as a chance to build real, working skills.
